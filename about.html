How TrailDistrict works.

TrailDistrict curates local businesses into experiences instead of another giant directory.

1. Pick a trail

Food, date night, coffee, vintage, arts & culture, and more.

2. Follow the stops

Each trail gives you a suggested order and an easy next step.

3. Discover locally

Try businesses you might not have found on your own.

4. Check in

Participating businesses can eventually use TrailDistrict QR/referral tracking.

For local businesses

TrailDistrict is testing a measurable local-discovery model. Founding businesses can receive trail placement and referral tracking during the pilot at no upfront cost.

Interested in the pilot?
Important: TrailDistrict does not guarantee customer volume. Business participation, offers, photos, descriptions, and promotional relationships must be approved before being represented as official.
